Join us for The Buzz About Banking

Tuesday 25 July 2023   |   5:30 — 7:30pm
Kiwibank Auckland Office, Wynyard Quarter

From picking up your morning coffee to buying that new outfit you’ve been saving for, you probably interact with your bank every single day. Join us to discover how you can grow your career at Kiwibank while shaping the future of banking in Aotearoa. At this event, you’ll hear from two of Kiwibank’s senior executives who lead their new tech innovations. Learn about their career journey and why banking is an exciting (and important!) sector.


You’ll also get your hands stuck into an innovation roadmap workshop for Kiwibank—what does innovation look like for the next 12 months? A new user interface? Faster processing? Mobile banking app features? It’s up to you and your teammates to find the best solution!
