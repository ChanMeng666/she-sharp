What’s been happening in May…

A word from Mahsa…

🌟 Kia ora  <<First Name>> 🌟


We’re almost halfway into the year and I must say, what a crazy year it has been! May has been an exhilarating month for She Sharp, filled with inspiring events and engaging connections! Let's dive into the highlights that made this month unforgettable.


Fitness and Innovation Unite! 🏋️‍♀️🧠

On May 9th, we teamed up with Les Mills New Zealand for an evening of fitness and innovation. Our speakers—Micayla Wright, Liz Miller, Effy Collis, Macy Ma, Rebecca Fox, Jessica Z., and Ramya Kommareddy—left us buzzing with new ideas and energy.


A Journey Across Sweden ✈️

From May 20th to 23rd, I travelled across Sweden, connecting with researchers and educators. We kicked off in Borås, discussing AI in education. In Varberg, we advocated for women in tech, and in Halmstad, we continued the conversation on diversity and inclusion in tech.

Owning (and doing) the unexpected with Les Mills International! 💪🏼

“Own the Unexpected” with Les Mills International was all about sharing, caring, and building connections. The Les Mills team and their fantastic speakers brought incredible energy, making the event as “unexpected” as promised—especially with the impromptu workout led by the amazing Rachel Newsham! We truly thank our inspiring speakers Micayla Wright, Liz Miller, Effy Collis, Macy Ma, Rebecca Fox, Jessica Z., and Ramya Kommareddy for sharing their unconventional routes to success in tech. A big shoutout to Amber Taylor and Les Mills International for making this event possible!
