Mind Your Own Career (MYOC) is fast approaching and we are introducing our amazing panel of speakers!

From the outside, when we think about tech, we think about CTOs, engineers, coding. This may be partly true but what about designers, quality assurance, product management? The technology industry requires an abundance of skills. It is constantly coming up with new roles to make existing processes more efficient.

Come along on Thursday, 28th April, 6:30 pm and engage with an expert team of MYOB panellists who will highlight some of the pathways that can be followed which lead to a career in Technology.

You will also get to participate in a guided workshop that will help you discover your deep-set motivations. Meet our Speakers!

Kyle Nicholas, Senior Developer at MYOB

Kyle is a Senior Developer at MYOB. He’s a classic T-Shaped Full Stack Developer and has worked with C#, Jade, PowerShell etc. He strongly believes that the journey is half the fun; traditional or non-traditional, that we learn from all of it and it’s what we make of it that counts

Taylor Waddington, Protégé Software Developer at MYOB

Taylor started at MYOB just over a year ago, in the Future Makers Academy program as a Protégé Software Developer. Before MYOB, she studied a double degree in Information Systems and Marketing in Dunedin. Since being at MYOB she’s had the opportunity to grow and navigate herself through the corporate world. She has discovered what it’s like to be a Software Developer and had the opportunity to venture outside of engineering and work aside one of MYOB’s Business Analyst to further develop her skills in that area!

Lyndsay Kilburn, Delivery Manager at MYOB

Lindsay has been with MYOB for 6 years across a variety of roles and is currently the Delivery Manager for a cross functional team of developers and engineers focused on site reliability engineering.

She's passionate about diversity in technology in all its forms and finding opportunities for people to thrive.
