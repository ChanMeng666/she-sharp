Join She Sharp & MYOB for International Women's Day, Thursday 11th March

Hi <<First Name>>,

She Sharp is delighted to kick-start 2021 with the celebration of International Women’s Day. We are partnering with MYOB for this event!

Please join us at the MYOB office located at 5/12 Normanby Rd, Level 5, Mt Eden on Thursday, 11th March at 6pm for an evening filled with inspiration, learning and networking.

It will be a fun night full of team challenges, quizzes and interactive awesomeness. So bring your A-game along! This is a great event to bring your work colleagues to network and reconnect with others you have not had the opportunity to meet in person over the last few months!

This event is open to all genders and ages, including high-school and university students as well as working professionals. There will be food and non-alcoholic drinks provided!

Meet our speakers

Camilla Weinstein, Talent Team Lead at MYOB and Director of Straw the Line NZ

Camila is a parent and came back to work during the Level 4 lockdown after her maternity leave. She is running a side hustle with a mission to save the planet. Camila will talk about what MYOB offers in terms of women network, support, parents hub, flexibility, and a new policy regarding Transgender challenging the status quo.

Grant Mclvor, Head of Technology, MYOB

Grant is passionate about delivering technology solutions and products that make a difference to users. He loves building and leading technology teams in pursuit of excellence and driving a high performance culture. At the event, Grant will be giving us insights into MYOB’s Future Makers Academy as well as discussing why he choose to challenge the gender balance with MYOB’s proteges.
