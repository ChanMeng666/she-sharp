March Recap 📝

A word from Mahsa…

🌟 Kia ora  <<First Name>> 🌟


April has been a month of deep learning, global collaboration, and inspiring conversations. As a Fulbright Scholar, I’ve continued my journey toward earning a Master Beekeeper certification here in North Carolina — discovering how AI and traditional beekeeping knowledge can come together to protect these vital pollinators. It’s a hands-on reminder that innovation is most powerful when it honors lived experience and wisdom.


This month also brought rich discussions around the future of work and the evolving role of AI in our lives. From digital transformation and the rise of non-degree credentials to the growing emphasis on emotional intelligence, it’s clear that adaptability and lifelong learning are becoming essential skills in every field. I was proud to lead several workshops focused on these themes — including multiple “Coding the Future” sessions and our She Sharp-hosted #IAmRemarkable workshop on April 16. This global movement empowers individuals, especially those from underrepresented groups, to embrace their achievements and challenge the biases around self-promotion. As we continue to navigate rapid change in both technology and society, it’s more important than ever to recognise our worth, speak our accomplishments out loud, and lift others as we rise.

Ngā mihi nui


Mahsa McCauley
Founder and Chair
She Sharp Charitable Trust

Last Event Update:

#IAmRemarkable

On April 16, 26 participants joined us online for She Sharp’s #IAmRemarkable workshop, led by our founder and director, Dr Mahsa McCauley (Mohaghegh), as part of a global movement to empower underrepresented voices.

The session created a safe and supportive space for attendees to reflect on their achievements, challenge biases around self-promotion, and grow in confidence. Energised by open conversations and shared stories, the group walked away inspired to celebrate their own value and uplift others in their communities.

We’re incredibly proud to bring this powerful initiative to our network and grateful to everyone who took part. Let’s keep the momentum going! 💬✨

Industry Gossip 🗣️

Check out what’s been going on in the tech world lately…

  • 🔍 Google Introduces AI Mode in Search for a Smarter Experience

    Google is revolutionizing the way we search online with the introduction of “AI Mode” in its Search platform. This new feature provides AI-generated answers sourced from Google’s search index, offering a more conversational and intuitive search experience. Unlike traditional search results, AI Mode delivers comprehensive answers, visual cards with detailed information about businesses and products, and saves past searches for easy revisiting. Currently available to a portion of U.S. users, this marks a significant step in integrating advanced AI into mainstream search functions. Source.


  • 📈 Global AI Spending to Surge by 60% in 2025

    A recent report by UBS projects that global artificial intelligence (AI) spending will rise by 60% in 2025, reaching $360 billion. Interestingly, while overall spending increases, the share attributed to major tech giants like Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, and Meta is expected to decline, falling from 58% in 2025 to 52% in 2026. This shift indicates a broader adoption of AI technologies across various industries and regions, highlighting the growing democratization and diversification of AI applications worldwide. Source.


  • 🌐 Sam Altman and Elon Musk Compete to Build the Ultimate ‘Everything App’

    Sam Altman and Elon Musk are in a race to develop a comprehensive “everything app” that integrates social media, finance, and more. Altman’s venture, World (formerly Worldcoin), utilizes iris-scanning technology to verify user identities, aiming to distinguish humans from AI bots. The World app functions as a crypto wallet, social network, and mini-app platform, boasting 26 million users, with 12 million verified through its Orb devices. Recent partnerships include collaborations with Visa, Stripe, and Match Group. Meanwhile, Musk’s X app is evolving into a financial and social hub, preparing to launch payment features in partnership with Visa. Both platforms seek to address the challenge of online identity verification in an AI-driven world. Source.

  • 📝 Sharing the recent article published by EdTechNZ  “Advancing Beekeeping with AI at North Carolina Agricultural and Technical State University”.

    As a Fulbright Visiting Scholar at Center of Excellence in Cybersecurity Research, Education and Outreach (CREO) , Dr Mahsa McCauley is  collaborating with Professor Hossein Sarrafzadeh and Dr. Iman Ardekani, PhD on the innovative Bee Doctor project. Their work integrates AI and machine learning to analyse bioacoustic signals from beehives, enabling early detection of diseases and environmental stressors affecting honeybee colonies. This technology allows for real-time monitoring and swift interventions to protect hive health, supporting both pollinator conservation and sustainable agriculture. Source.
