Join She Sharp & Fergus on 29th April in Moving Beyond Diversity to Inclusivity

“Diversity is an invitation to the party, inclusion is about an invitation to dance” - Verna Myers

She sharp is delighted to be partnering with Fergus to bring you this month’s event which is all about shining the spotlight on women in tech and trades who are proving they are more than just a diversity quota filler.

We will also be accompanied by industry leaders who have not settled for a seat on the sidelines and are setting a new benchmark for inclusivity every day.

Please join us at the Fergus Office located on 65 New North Road, Eden Terrace, Auckland, on Thursday, 29th April at 6:00pm for an evening filled with discussion, inspiration and networking. There will also be fun activities and quizzes to keep you engaged!

This event is open to all genders and ages, including high-school and university students as well as working professionals. There will be food and non-alcoholic beverages provided

Meet your speakers

Amy FitzPatrick, Founder and Qualified Electrician, The Pink Sparky

Having started her apprenticeship in 2004, Amy is now the Founder and Director of Pink Sparky. She currently employs, trains and supports 2 female apprentices on their path to being fully qualified. Amy and her team are based in The Waikato and fully support Women in Trades

Chris Stevens, Chief Technology and Product Officer at Fergus

Chris is the newly appointed CTPO at Fergus, leading their engineering, product and design teams. Chris is a SaaS veteran, having worked for several global success stories such as PushPay, Orion Health and most recently, AskNicely. What's even better is his passion for building awesome software that delivers for its customers, the hard-working trades using Fergus being the best example.

Ruby Kolesky, Co-Chief Executive Officer & Heart of Product at Joyus

Only one in four people working in the tech industry are women. That stat is not going to improve until companies get better at supporting all of the parents that work for them. Ruby is one of the 25%. She is the Co-CEO and Heart of Product at Joyous. Her experiences being a mother in a male-dominated industry has led her to create an open source parental journey policy and benefit that any business can afford.

Babra Ajaz, Junior Software Engineer at Fergus Software

Babra is a recent graduate from the University of Auckland. She studied Computer Systems Engineering and is working as a Software Engineer at Fergus Software. Her journey in the technical field, although just starting, has been guided with strong mentors that encourage her every day to be her best self and not let any biases come in the way.
