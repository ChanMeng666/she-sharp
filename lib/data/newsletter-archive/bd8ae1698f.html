March Recap 📝

A word from Mahsa…

🌟 Kia ora  <<First Name>> 🌟


Our first event of the year celebrating International Women’s Day at academyEX was truly special! Although I couldn't join in person this year, I'm deeply grateful to our incredible She Sharp team for creating such a meaningful gathering. The theme, #AccelerateAction, served as a powerful reminder of the ongoing journey toward gender equality. Huge thanks to our inspiring speakers—Perrin Rowland, Moumita Das Roy and Janet Van Jenkins —who motivated and empowered us to continue driving impactful change in technology and beyond.

I also recently attended a virtual session with Tony Robbins and wanted to share some powerful insights that resonated deeply with me:

✨ "Where focus goes, energy flows."

🎯 "The past does not equal the future."

🌱 "Change must become a MUST, not a should."

Remember, someone out there is waiting for YOUR story. Be their spark, step up, and unleash the power within!

I'm thrilled to invite you to our next event—#IAmRemarkable—on April 16, 2025. This global movement empowers everyone, especially underrepresented groups, to confidently celebrate their achievements while challenging societal perceptions around self-promotion. Join me for this empowering 60-minute workshop—I can’t wait to see you there!

Ngā mihi nui


Mahsa McCauley

Last Event Update:

International Women’s Day 2025

with She Sharp and academyEX

On March 14, nearly 60 people gathered at academyEX for She Sharp's International Women’s Day event, coming together to celebrate, reflect, and uplift one another.

The room was energized with deep conversations, inspiring stories, and meaningful connections. Speakers Moumita Das Roy, Perrin Rowland, and Janet Van Jenkins prompted valuable reflections on resilience, generosity, and collective success. Dr Mahsa McCauley (Mohaghegh) joined virtually from the U.S. to deliver a powerful keynote on intentional progress in diversity, equity, and inclusion, emphasizing the global necessity of these conversations.

We're incredibly grateful to academyEX, our ambassadors, volunteers, and everyone who attended. Here's to continued progress and breaking barriers together! 💜🙌

Industry Gossip 🗣️

Check out what’s been going on in the tech world lately…

  • Jon Hartley, an economist at Stanford University, co-authored a paper titled “The Labor Market Effects of Generative Artificial Intelligence,” published recently. It’s a bit of an academic read, but some interesting findings like the usage of AI among uni vs non uni students.

    You can access the full paper on SSRN.


  • Microsoft is developing its own in-house AI reasoning models (which are reportedly comparable to OpenAI’s o1 and o3 mini-reasoning models) and searching for an alternative model, other than OpenAI’s GPT, to power its CoPilot chatbot, to reduce its reliance on the start-up, despite previously being its biggest investor. Source.


  • LinkedIn Is Testing an AI Tool That Could Transform How People Search for Jobs. The company says AI could help surface jobs that remain hidden from typical search queries. Job searchers can enter queries such as “find me a role where I can use marketing skills to help the environment,” or “show jobs in marketing that pay over $100K.”

    A regular search might only bring up openings based on their job title; the new tool can identify ones based on a deeper analysis of the job description, information about the company and its peers, and posts from across the site. It can also show job seekers what new skills they might need to pursue in order to land a particular role. Source.
