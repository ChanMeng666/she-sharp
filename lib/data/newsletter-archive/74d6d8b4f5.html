Tēnā koe <<First Name>>


This Month, we celebrated the incredible achievements of women at our 2023 She Sharp kick off event at CountdownX. It was inspiring to see so many women come together to share their stories and inspire one another. We've come a long way in the fight for gender equality, but there's still so much work to be done. Growing up in Iran has shaped and made me who I am today. I might not have had the same motivation to pursue success, to be determined to push myself to the same extent. Seeing the many brave women in Iran fighting out against oppression, fighting for their basic human rights, I am proud to be an Iranian woman.


Let's keep pushing forward, challenging gender stereotypes, and empowering women to achieve their full potential.


Here's to a future where all women can thrive! We are looking forward to seeing you in our next month event at MYOB.


Ngā mihi


Mahsa McCauley

Founder and Chair She Sharp Charitable Trust



Upcoming Event


She Sharp & MYOB - Hearing from Opportunities at MYOB for a Career in Tech (DevelopHer) Program


We are thrilled to announce an upcoming event in partnership with MYOB. This event is aimed at providing women with insights and opportunities to pursue a career in the tech industry, and we can't wait to share it with you!


The event will be held on April 28th at the MYOB office in Mt Eden. Attendees will have the chance to hear from experienced professionals in the tech industry, who will be sharing their personal stories, experiences and insights on how to navigate a career in tech. This is a unique opportunity to gain valuable insights and advice from some of the best in the industry, and to learn more about MYOB as a company.


Whether you're looking to start a career in tech, or you're already working in the industry and looking to grow your skills and network, this event is for you. With the support of MYOB, we hope to inspire and empower women to pursue their passions and achieve their career goals.

We will have special prizes and giveaways for those who register and attend the event.


Update on She Sharp


Past Event: International Women's Day Breakfast Celebration


Our recent International Women's Day Breakfast Celebration event was a huge success and a great way to kick off our events for the year. We were grateful to partner with Countdown to bring this event to life, and we want to extend our sincere thanks to them for their support.


The event was an incredible morning filled with inspiration, networking, and delicious food. We had the pleasure of hearing from some amazing speakers, who shared their experiences and insights on navigating the challenges and opportunities of being a woman in STEM. We also had the opportunity to connect with other women in our community, and partake in informative exercises with one another.


We are so thankful to everyone who attended and helped make this event such a success. Your energy, enthusiasm, and support are what make our community so special. We can't wait to build on this momentum and continue to create opportunities for women to connect, learn, and grow. It was such a memorable and inspiring event!
