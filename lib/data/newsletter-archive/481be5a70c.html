This month we have been super busy planning for our exciting upcoming events! The AI Hackathon is shaping up to be an incredible event. This is the first hackathon organised by She Sharp, and the first multi-day event we’ve had in a while, and the team are super excited to bring it to you!

Upcoming Events

AI For the Environment - Female Led Hackathon

She Sharp & the AI Forum are excited to present the first-ever female-led AI hackathon! Join us over the 3-4th September 2022 for a weekend of creative problem-solving in a supportive female-led environment.

Check out this press release from AI Forum NZ about why we’re running this event, and the need to close the gender gap in AI - https://nztech.org.nz/2022/08/24/nz-must-close-the-gender-gap-in-ai/

She Sharp Hackathon is an all-female-led and non-binary 32-hour hackathon, which aims to help individuals to understand they belong in tech and that there are various roles you can have in the tech industry. Join 60+ people for a weekend of learning, building, and connecting to build your tech skills and realise that you have allies who are supporting you every step of the way.

There will be two winning teams that will go forward to present their solution on centre stage at the Aotearoa AI Summit Conference on 12 September! There will be other prizes up for grabs!
