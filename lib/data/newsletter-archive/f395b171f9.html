International Women’s Day (IWD) is a concept that is older than a century. The first time it was celebrated was 1911 in Copenhagen, Denmark with more than a million attendees. Year on year, March 8 is earmarked for the observance of all the women around us.

This year’s IWD theme is #BreakTheBias. The community is talking about a gender equal world. A world free of bias, stereotypes and discrimination. A world that's diverse, equitable, and inclusive. A world where difference is valued and celebrated.

The team at She Sharp are also taking this moment, to bring you all together and talk about breaking the bias in the tech sector. We’ve put together an online event for you on Thursday, 10th March at 6:30pm.

The evening is going to start with fire chats about breaking the bias with panelists who have different levels of experience. This will lead into a workshop about a hot topic nowadays, ✨ NFT ✨ creation. We’re going to end the evening with a short quiz where the winner gets an AllBirds voucher of $160. There’s 2 more nights and limited spaces!
