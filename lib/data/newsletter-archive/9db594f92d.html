Introducing our amazing speakers!

SAI HONIG (NZNWS)

Sai Honig is a multipotentialite who has worked in differing roles in various industries. Prior to information security, Sai was an aerospace design engineer and financial/ operational auditing. Sai’s IT experience is in the governance, audit, supply chain, risk management, and security.

DIVYA KATARIA (TRANSMISSION GULLY)

Divya is currently working as a Graduate Bridge Engineer at Transmission Gully. She is a UN Peace Ambassador in New Zealand. Her life's mission is to continue helping the community through her skills and civil engineering degree.
