A word from Mahsa…

🌟 Kia ora  <<First Name>> 🌟


As 2025 draws to a close, one truth stands out: the future belongs to those who choose to build it together.


This has been a year of courage, curiosity, and collaboration of stepping into new rooms, asking braver questions, and trusting that when people, ideas, and technology come together with intention, transformative things can happen.


Across borders and time zones, a single thread has connected every conversation: human values must sit at the heart of innovation. As we look ahead, may we carry forward this spirit of partnership; continuing to learn, to share, and to tackle meaningful problems side by side.


Reach out, stay curious, and keep stretching toward the next skill, the next idea, the next possibility that only emerges when we create it together.

Ngā mihi nui


Mahsa McCauley
Founder and Chair
She Sharp Charitable Trust

Last Events Update

 

Future-Ready: Data, AI & Digital Innovation at Vector

✨Our collaboration with Vector Limited was a celebration of innovation and community. Together, we hosted 'Future-Ready', an evening dedicated to exploring Data, AI, and inclusive careers, filled with incredible energy and creativity.


​The event featured a standout panel of Vector women—including Karen Ip, Shona Irving, Adriana Yong, and Evelyn Hunsberger—who shared honest, inspiring stories from their tech journeys. Participants also dove into a fast-paced "think-a-thon," pitching crowdsourcing app concepts focused on resilience during storms and evolving customer engagement.


​We witnessed the power of connection in action, with insightful conversations leaving everyone feeling empowered. A huge thank you to Mark Toner for MCing, and to Jenna Roake and the team for organizing such an outstanding effort.

Shaping Tomorrow: She Sharp Launches in Dunedin

✨ Our inaugural She Sharp event in Dunedin at the University of Otago marked a significant milestone for our community. With the generous sponsorship of HCLTech and support from the Dunedin City Council, we successfully set the stage for future events across the South Island.


​Keynote speaker Rasha Y. Abu-Safieh delivered powerful insights on the shifting landscape of work by 2030. She was joined by industry leaders Graeme Riley, Deb Sutton, Sophie Wiltshier, and Kerry Ryan, who challenged us to prioritize ethics and human connection amidst rapid technological change. Expertly moderated by Steve Gadsby, the discussions emphasized that skills like creativity and critical thinking remain our strongest assets.

​

The event also shone a light on the Otago Digital Internship Program, which is creating equitable pathways for neurodiverse and disabled communities. A heartfelt thank you to our speakers, partners, and the dedicated She Sharp team for making this launch unforgettable.

Industry Gossips

 


Hollywood Reporter Big Lawsuit Targets Major AI Companies Over Copyright


A major new legal showdown just dropped: New York Times reporter John Carreyrou — famous for exposing Theranos — has filed a lawsuit against major AI companies including OpenAI, Google, Meta, xAI, and Anthropic, accusing them of using copyrighted books to train their chatbots without permission. The case rejects class actions in favor of individual claims and could spark a wave of similar copyright battles across the industry, forcing AI firms to rethink training data practices.

Nvidia’s Major Power Play: Acquires SchedMD to Own the AI Supercomputing Backbone



In a strategic shift that has the AI world buzzing, Nvidia just snapped up SchedMD, the company behind Slurm — the open-source workload manager that orchestrates massive computing jobs for AI and supercomputing clusters. The deal signals Nvidia’s intent to go beyond chips and cement its grip on the software infrastructure that powers next-gen AI research and deployment.
