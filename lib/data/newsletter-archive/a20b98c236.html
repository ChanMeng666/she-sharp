October is here and for this month, we are celebrating Ada Lovelace day and International Day of the Girl. Both of these occasions hold equal importance in our community.

Ada Lovelace was an English mathematician and writer and has been called the first computer programmer. She was alive in the 1800s and has been remembered ever since.

International Day of the Girl was created by the United Nation in 2012, almost 10 years ago now, to celebrate the achievements and potentials of girls.

Who’s speaking?

Ankita Dhakar, Managing Director at Security Lit

Due to the impact of COVID-19 and people moving to remote working and more businesses choosing digital solutions brought a cyber-pandemic to New Zealand. Once Ankita learnt about cyber security incidents and the very real threats companies and individuals face in the digital era, she knew she had to help.

With this vision, she founded Security Lit, A company providing penetration testing and security consultancy services to SMEs. Ankita made it her mission to make cybersecurity solutions easy and affordable so that all businesses – big or small - can operate confidently and securely in cyberspace.

Elina Ashimbayeva, Co-creator of Storyo

Elina whakapapa to Kazakhstan in Central Asia and has a massive keen-bean-ness (read: heart) for social justice, equity and diversity. She has worked in biomedical research, start-ups, government, and is now studying human rights. In 2019, together with her partner, Elina started an online platform called Storyo, where they've interviewed 100+ women and gender diverse folks across Aotearoa, showcasing vulnerability and changing the narrative of what success means in modern culture.
