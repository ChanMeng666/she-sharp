What happened in October?

A word from Mahsa…

Kia ora <<First Name>>


We’re in the last leg of 2023 — can you believe it? It has been a busy year for She Sharp, and October was no different. This month, we hosted our first ever conference for young Māori and Pasifika women in Years 11-13: INSPIRE HER: TE WHAKATIPURANGA WAHINE and it was a huge success! 🥳


With over 100 students attending, the students gathered at the AUT South Campus in Manukau. There, they gained invaluable insights from our incredible keynote speakers who shared inspiring and motivating stories for the girls. 💭


Hands-on workshops allowed the students to see electrical engineering, augmented reality, robotics, virtual reality and more in action! 🤖 We were also lucky to have industry representatives from Fonterra, MYOB and others who showed the girls the careers they could pursue in the future. 👩‍🔬


We extend our gratitude to AUT School of Engineering, Computer and Mathematical Sciences for generously providing full scholarships for three students to pursue any engineering major! 🎓 This really helps turn a fun day of learning into tangible opportunities for these young women to break down barriers in STEM. 🫂


💡Learn more about AUT’s scholarships here.


A big shoutout to our sponsors, including AUT, Google, Māngere-Ōtāhuhu Local Board, and Whau Local Board, for making this event possible! 💜


The energy and excitement of the day reinforced the profound impact of our mission to create a more diverse STEM community.


This is just the beginning! ✨

Ngā mihi nui


Mahsa McCauley

With a Bachelor of Electrical Computer Engineering, Sara saw the gender gap firsthand when studying and believes an innovative future in tech comes from real diversity. Sara’s passionate about using AI for the greater good, studying a Master of Computer and Information Science at Auckland University of Technology.
