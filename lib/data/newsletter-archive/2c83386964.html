She Sharp x Tech Babes NZ - THRIVE: Your Career, Your Story

🌟 Kia ora  <<First Name>> 🌟

Feeling like job hunting is a full-time job in itself? You're not alone—and we’ve all been there. Whether you're gearing up to graduate, freshly out of uni, or thinking about your next big career move, the job search can feel like a maze. But here’s the good news: you don’t have to navigate it solo. This July, your career hype squad, She Sharp and TechBabesNZ, are joining forces to bring you an event designed to give your job hunt the glow-up it deserves.

📍 HPE, Level 19 PWC Tower, Commercial Bay, 15 Customs Street West, Auckland Central

📅 Wednesday, 30 July | 5:00 PM – 7:30 PM


Are you a soon-to-be graduate, a recent graduate, or a professional ready for a career move?


This event is designed just for you. Join us for an empowering session focused on helping you take the next step in your career journey. Whether you're searching for your first job or exploring a career transition, this event will equip you with the tools, insights, and confidence you need.


We’ll cover key topics including:

Personal Branding: Learn how to present yourself confidently and authentically in the job market.

Assessing CVs: Get expert feedback on how to strengthen your CV to stand out from the crowd.

Interview Strategies: Discover practical tips to prepare for and succeed in interviews.

Mentorship Guidance: Hear from experienced professionals and gain valuable career advice tailored to your goals.


This is more than a talk — it’s a hands-on, interactive experience designed to help you build clarity, confidence, and connections. Open to all, but especially geared towards university students, recent graduates, and professionals seeking new opportunities.
