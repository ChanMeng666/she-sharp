July Recap 📝

A word from Mahsa…

🌟 Kia ora  <<First Name>> 🌟

From standing alongside fearless young leaders at The Girl Empowerment Conference, to engaging with global innovators at the China Business Summit, this month has been all about witnessing and contributing to change that matters.

At TGEC, I was reminded of the power of youth-led spaces - where Year 13 women tackled business challenges, shared openly about identity and imposter syndrome, and showed us what leadership looks like when courage meets community. Their vision left me hopeful and deeply proud.

Later in Auckland, the China Business Summit offered a chance to learn about China’s rapidly evolving AI landscape and to reflect on the unique opportunities for collaboration between New Zealand’s agile innovators and China’s global-scale technology leaders. The conversations were thought-provoking and underscored the importance of partnership in shaping our shared future.

This month also marked a milestone for Aotearoa: the launch of our first national AI Strategy. It’s a bold step forward, ensuring businesses can harness AI safely and effectively to drive productivity and impact. Being part of these pivotal conversations reaffirmed how crucial it is to pair vision with action.

And now, I’m looking ahead with excitement as She Sharp and AUT prepare to host the 2025 AI Hackathon in August. With only five years left to achieve the SDGs - and most targets off track - bringing together diverse minds to design real-world AI solutions feels both urgent and inspiring.

July has reminded me that progress is not just about policy or technology - it’s about people stepping up, connecting, and daring to lead. And the best part? This journey is only just beginning.

Ngā mihi nui


Mahsa McCauley
Founder and Chair
She Sharp Charitable Trust

Check out what’s been going on in the tech world lately…

  • 🇳🇿 New Zealand Launches First National AI Strategy

    New Zealand  unveiled its first-ever AI Strategy: “Investing with Confidence”, focused on accelerating safe and effective AI adoption. The strategy provides voluntary Responsible AI Guidance for businesses and the public sector, aiming to boost productivity while following a light-touch, principles-based regulatory approach.

    Source.

  • 📚 OpenAI Launches “Study Mode” in ChatGPT

    OpenAI introduced Study Mode, a new feature in ChatGPT designed to help students build critical thinking skills. Instead of just giving answers, ChatGPT now asks questions to test understanding and, in some cases, withholds direct responses until students engage with the material.

    Source.

  • 📱 Apple iOS 26 Public Beta Arrives

    Apple released the first public beta of iOS 26, introducing its boldest redesign in over a decade. Featuring a sleek “Liquid Glass” visual language across the interface and deeper integration of Apple Intelligence, the update enhances core apps like Phone, Messages, Camera, and Music pllus a brand‑new Games app. Early adopters now get an exclusive preview ahead of the official launch expected later this fall.
