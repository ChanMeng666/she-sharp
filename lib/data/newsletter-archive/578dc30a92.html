Recent Event Updates


Making LinkedIn Work for You



On 15 May, She Sharp teamed up with the Auckland University of Technology (AUT) to host an insightful evening dedicated to mastering professional networking: Making LinkedIn Work for You.


Led by Stuart Little (Creative Strategist and Director of Agency8), the workshop provided a practical walkthrough on how to build a LinkedIn profile that attracts the right career opportunities. Stuart emphasized the power of authenticity, showing participants how to confidently showcase their skills, personal brand, and evidence of learning—like portfolios and project videos—to stand out to industry professionals.


The session also featured Janelle Wright, who shared valuable insider tips on the recruitment process, strategic job hunting, and exactly what employers are looking for when they scan a digital CV.


Key Takeaways from the Evening:

  • Your profile is your digital first impression: Authenticity and consistency are the fastest ways to improve your professional visibility.

  • Show, don't just tell: Adding multimedia (like projects or videos) provides tangible proof of your technical capabilities and personal strengths.

  • Engage to grow: Actively sharing your learning journey and interacting with others’ content is crucial for building a meaningful network.


Overall, the attendees left feeling inspired and equipped with actionable strategies to take control of their online presence and career development.



Cybersecurity Awareness: Building Resilient Digital Workplaces



The HER WAKA – Navigating Pathways into Sustainable Employment session, held on Tuesday, 5 May 2026, at academyEX in Auckland, formed the May edition of the event series. Delivered by She Sharp in partnership with the Ministry of Social Development, the programme brought together up to 25 participants in a structured, high-impact environment focused on strengthening pathways into employment. The session emphasised building job readiness, confidence, and practical understanding of the New Zealand labour market.


Participants gained a clearer understanding of the evolving job market, with a specific focus on cybersecurity awareness and the growing demand for skills that support safer and more resilient digital workplaces. The session provided practical insights into employer expectations, recruitment trends, and the challenges within the broader tech employment landscape. Participants were supported with practical skills and employer guidance to better position themselves confidently and competitively.


The session fostered a supportive and inclusive environment where participants engaged in open conversations and shared experiences. A panel discussion featuring industry experts Paula Gair, Swapna Soni, and Prasanth Pavithran, alongside contributions from Sian Clements and Jenny Martin representing RCSA, provided real-world perspectives on hiring expectations and workplace trends. By sharing their career journeys and key lessons, the speakers created a space where participants were able to build confidence, develop communication skills, and gain clarity on their employment pathways.



The programme included a combination of panel discussions and a 45-minute structured networking activity designed to translate insights into action. Three dedicated discussion tables were set up, allowing participants to engage directly with recruiters and panel speakers, ask questions, and practice real-world professional conversations. The session concluded with this engaging networking segment, enabling participants to build connections and leave better prepared, informed, and confident in navigating their next steps towards sustainable employment.
