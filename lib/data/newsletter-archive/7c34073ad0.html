When we look for speakers, we look for people who have truly walked the path.


That is why we are so excited to introduce Candice Murray, who will be leading our upcoming session on April 16.


Candice Murray is a Career Mindset Coach and one of Influence Digest Media’s Top Coaches in Auckland for 2025 and 2024. Her workshops have been delivered for AWS, Salesforce, Spark NZ, AUT University and many more great kiwi organisations.


With her background in technology including five years at Microsoft, she gets your world.  She talks about her journey with debilitating nerves and lack of confidence and shows you the techniques she used to improve, one step at a time.


In her Own Your Energy workshop you’ll choose the energy you need more of in your life and career right now:  confidence, calm, self-belief or focus. You’ll learn simple, science-backed tools to strengthen your chosen energy and embody it in those important moments when you need it most.


It's a fun, safe space to normalise that we all feel a lack of confidence, self-belief or calm at least some of the time.  It's what you do about it that matters ... learning tools to step into the best version of yourself at will is the power move!
