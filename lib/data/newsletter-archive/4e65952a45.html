She Sharp and the AI Forum are running the first ever Female-led Hackathon aimed to empower and engage women by giving them a supportive learning environment to show the roles that they can have in technology.

How does it work?
You can join as an individual participant or as part of team.
A team is like a squad, made up of people that have different skills and capabilities (this could be a BA, Product Designer, UI/UX Designer, Software Engineer, Data Analyst, Data Scientist, Non Technical person). The hackathon will focus on a specific theme - AI for Good, for the environment. The published report AI for the Environment outlines 10 recommendations divided into 3 sections.

The day of the event
You should arrive at the event with a project idea, your laptop and bundles of enthusiasm.
Each person who has a project idea, you may not at thats ok too, will be given 2 mins to pitch this to the other hackers. Other participants choose to join your team or you may choose to join another persons/teams project idea.

Each team is made up of 5 people, over the course of the hack, you will work together to build a solution, (mentors and coaches will be available to support you). On the Sunday afternoon your team will pitch to the judging panel.

The winning teams will pitch their solutions at the AI Summit on 12 September in Auckland, where the overall winners will be announced.

This hackathon is an all-female-led and non-binary 32-hour hackathon, to help individuals understand they belong in tech and that there are various roles you can have in the tech industry.

Please join us and 60+ people on the weekend of the 3rd September, 9:00am to 4th September 5:00pm.

It will be a weekend full of learning new and cool things to add to your tech arsenal and realise that you have allies who are supporting you every step of the way. Secure your tickets now!
