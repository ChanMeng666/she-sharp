Update on She Sharp

This month we have been busy planning for our exciting Christmas event, as well as 2023. The team at She Sharp are super excited to bring you a celebration of the year to come, as well as some sneak peaks for what we’re going to be up to next year!

We were also honoured to be awarded the Best Industry Initiative that Supports Diversity, Inclusion and Equality at the recent Women in Security awards. We are truly grateful to be recognised for the work that we have been doing.

Keep an eye out on our socials for more information about what we’ve got coming up! We’re on Instagram (@shesharpnz), Facebook, Twitter (@SheSharpNZ) and LinkedIn, and you can join our Facebook group to stay connected.

SheSharp is delighted to present She Celebrates: A reflection on the past year and Inspiration for the future! We are excited to see you there!
