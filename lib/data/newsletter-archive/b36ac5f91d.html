New Zealand faces a growing skills shortage in cybersecurity and women have a vital role to play in shaping its future. That’s why SheSharp, Secure Code Warrior, and Xero are joining forces to bring you an empowering, hands-on workshop built exclusively for women and female-identifying participants.


📅 Date: Friday, October 3, 2025
🕙 Time: 9:30 AM – 2:00 PM NZST (Registration starts at 9:30 AM; event proper starts at 10:00 AM)
📍 Venue: Xero (Auckland)


Here’s what’s in store:


✅ Secure Code Warrior Workshop — gamified coding challenges to explore real-world secure coding scenarios (beginner-friendly, with pseudocode options, through to advanced).


✅ Lightning Talks — hear from four inspiring women leaders who are paving the way in tech and cybersecurity:

  • Fatemah Beydoun – Chief Customer Officer, Secure Code Warrior
    A founding team member at Secure Code Warrior, Fatemah leads global customer success and brings deep expertise from her work in cybersecurity across government, industry, and community.

  • Suzy Clark – Executive GM of Security, Xero
    With 26 years in technology across the UK and NZ, Suzy drives security at Xero while bringing a wealth of experience in senior leadership and technical security.

  • Dr. Mahsa McCauley – Founder, She Sharp
    A renowned advocate for women in technology, Mahsa has dedicated her career to mentoring, research, and community initiatives that empower women to succeed in STEM.

  • Mehika Manocha – Lead Engineer, Xero
    A leader in both engineering and diversity advocacy, Mehika champions secure practices and inclusive culture at Xero while supporting initiatives like Code Like a Girl.

✅ Collaboration & Community — connect with others, learn together, and be part of a movement closing the gender gap in cybersecurity.


Whether you’re just curious, starting out, or ready to sharpen your secure coding skills, this workshop is your opportunity to grow your confidence, build practical skills, and connect with a supportive community of women leading the charge.
