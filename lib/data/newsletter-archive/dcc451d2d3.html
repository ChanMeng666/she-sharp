She Sharp is Back for 2025!

A word from Mahsa…

🌟 Kia ora  <<First Name>> 🌟


As we continue to push boundaries in tech and innovation, I’m excited to share some incredible milestones and reflections from the past month.
Time really flies—this year has already been a busy one!I’m two months into my Fulbright research program at North Carolina Agricultural and Technical State University, working on AI-driven beehive health analysis. It’s an honor to collaborate with brilliant minds and contribute to this groundbreaking project. The potential impact of technology on environmental sustainability is truly inspiring.
In the spirit of innovation, I want to extend a huge congratulations to the team behind Buzzly, one of the winners of the World Summit Awards 2024. 
Their work in government and citizen engagement is a testament to how technology can drive positive societal change. As a national expert for WSA, I am beyond proud to see New Zealand innovators making a global impact!
Finally, I’m thrilled to share that SheSharp is welcoming new sponsors, further expanding our support network for women in tech. If you’re interested in learning more about what we’ve been doing together with our amazing sponsors, check out the sponsorship proposal in this letter. As we move forward, I encourage each of you to stay curious, keep learning, and never underestimate the impact of your work.


Let’s continue shaping 
the future together!



Ngā mihi nui


Mahsa McCauley
Founder and Chair
She Sharp Charitable Trust
