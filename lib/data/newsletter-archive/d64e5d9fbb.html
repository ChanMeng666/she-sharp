September Recap 📝

A word from Mahsa…

🌟 Kia ora  <<First Name>> 🌟




September has been a month of significant global connection, local milestones, and powerful reminders of AI’s potential for good.



The momentum of AI innovation in Aotearoa was showcased through the Aotearoa AI Forum Awards and the subsequent Aotearoa AI Summit. What stood out most was the unwavering commitment to ensuring AI is a force for good, serving our businesses, communities, and whānau. The first ever AI Forum Awards set a wonderful tone, celebrating remarkable innovators and teams pushing boundaries together.


The Summit delivered a true milestone for New Zealand with Minister Shane Reti MP's announcement of up to $70 million for AI research and commercialisation, signaling a transformative commitment to building a thriving, trustworthy AI ecosystem. I am grateful to the AI Forum NZ Executive Council and Executive Director Madeline Newman for their leadership in making this momentum possible.


I was also delighted to be a keynote speaker at the NZ Government Summit, discussing how Digital Transformation and AI are practical levers for leadership and the future of work in the public sector.


On a global note, I had a rewarding time teaching at China Jiliang University in Hangzhou, where students made impressive progress in advanced IoT applications. This was followed by the Apsara Conference 2025, which offered a fascinating view of cutting-edge global innovations in AI agents and robotics. The event gave me a close-up view of how Alibaba Cloud and China are shaping the future of these technologies.


From Tāmaki Makaurau to Hangzhou, the key takeaway is the same: purpose-driven innovation and strong community collaboration are essential. Let’s keep building on this momentum together.

Ngā mihi nui


Mahsa McCauley
Founder and Chair
She Sharp Charitable Trust

Last Event Update

Business and Technology Transformation Through Platforms and Products

✨What a fantastic evening of connection, learning, and inspiration at the Business and Technology Transformation Through Platforms and Products event, brought to you by She Sharp and Fonterra on September 3rd!

The event featured a dynamic panel discussion with three experienced wāhine leaders—Tracey Connor, Aileen Collins, and Kritika Law—who shared powerful insights into how Fonterra is shifting toward a product and platform-centric approach to drive greater business outcomes. We were also honoured to host special guest speaker Enkhsaikhan Vicky, who shared her inspiring journey as a women-in-tech advocate and GenAI expert.

The night was a huge success, complete with networking, kai, a competitive quiz, and even an iconic karaoke performance of an original Fonterra song! The biggest takeaway was clear: fun, play, and technology go hand in hand, and when they do, true transformation happens.

Ngā mihi nui to all our speakers, including emcee Jazz Sampat and Quiz Master Sarah Withers, as well as to Fonterra CIO Toby Granwal, our founder Dr. Mahsa McCauley, and the incredible volunteers from both the She Sharp and Fonterra teams for making this collaboration so successful.

Here’s to more connection, learning, and being #GoodTogether 💜

Industry Gossip 🗣️


Check out what’s been going on in the tech world lately…

The pace of innovation and infrastructure investment in the AI world continues to accelerate, with two major trends dominating the headlines this month: the colossal scale of AI infrastructure deals and Amazon's pivot to hardware showcasing GenAI-powered consumer devices.

🚀 The Trillion-Dollar AI Infrastructure Race


The AI arms race has escalated to a new, breathtaking scale, marked by unprecedented spending on the computing power required to build next-generation models.

The 'Stargate' Project: A consortium including OpenAI, Oracle, and SoftBank has unveiled aggressive plans for the $500 billion 'Stargate' initiative. This project aims to establish the largest AI infrastructure network in history, starting with five new data centers in the U.S. that will add nearly 7 gigawatts of compute capacity.

Mega-Deals: This commitment is underscored by other massive partnerships, notably Nvidia's $100 billion alliance with OpenAI to build 10 gigawatts of AI clusters, and Microsoft's $80 billion commitment to AI-enabled data centers in FY 2025.

The Bottom Line: These investments signal AI’s shift from an experimental tool to the bedrock economy for major tech giants, highlighting that the race for superior computational power is now the ultimate competitive advantage.

💡 Amazon's GenAI-Powered Hardware Push


Amazon is signaling a major return to hardware innovation, with a strategic focus on integrating its newly enhanced Alexa Plus generative AI into consumer devices.

Fall Event Focus: The company has scheduled a major Devices & Services event for September 30, where it's expected to showcase new Echo smart speakers, Fire TV devices, and Kindle updates.

The Alexa Plus Effect: Under new leadership, Amazon is moving past software-only updates to reveal hardware capable of truly utilizing the generative AI enhancements in Alexa Plus, potentially including beefed-up processing power for on-device actions.

What to Expect: Speculation points to updates like a potential colour version of the Kindle Scribe and faster, smarter Echo devices, showcasing how GenAI is being integrated into the everyday experience of smart home technology.

🏘️ The Great Land Grab: Billionaires vs. Communities


A critical and often contentious topic is sparking debate across the tech world: the move by Silicon Valley billionaires to buy up vast tracts of land to build futuristic, private cities and communities.

This trend is moving beyond philanthropy to large-scale, private urban planning, creating tension with existing residents.

The Central Conflict: These ambitious projects, often pitched as sustainable utopias powered by advanced technology, face fierce opposition from local communities. Critics argue that these secretive land purchases and proposed developments threaten local agriculture, housing affordability, and water resources, and often bypass democratic planning processes.

The Ethical Question: The debate centers on whether the immense wealth and visionary ambition of a few should be allowed to radically reshape local economies and ecosystems without proper public consent. It raises questions about who holds the power to design the future—and for whom.

A Shift in Focus: This signals a trend where the world's wealthiest technologists are shifting their focus from purely digital innovation to controlling physical space, aiming to build real-world labs for their long-term vision of societal change.

This conversation reminds us that technological ambition must always be balanced with ethical responsibility, genuine community engagement, and democratic governance.
