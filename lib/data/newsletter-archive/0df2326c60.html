October Recap 📝

A word from Mahsa…

🌟 Kia ora  <<First Name>> 🌟



October has been extraordinary for the She Sharp community—a month that captured exactly what we’re about: positioning women at the forefront of technology in Aotearoa.


We kicked things off with Code Secure. Lead the Future: Women in Cybersecurity Workshop on 3 October. Partnering with Xero and Secure Code Warrior, we created something special—a hands-on security learning experience where talented women tackled gamified challenges with confidence and skill. Watching this unfold filled me with pride.


SheSharp has proven, unequivocally, that the future of cybersecurity in New Zealand is vibrant and unstoppable.


Mid-October brought our presence at the Connecting the Dots webinar, where we continued championing diversity as essential to strengthening Aotearoa’s tech landscape.


The final weeks gave us critical global perspective. My return from SXSW Sydney reinforced something I’ve long believed: the future isn’t about choosing between human ingenuity and artificial intelligence—it’s about composing them together.


This thinking shaped my contribution at the 6D AI Auckland 25 Conference on 21 October, where we examined the vital need for rigorous content validation to ensure AI accuracy and safety.


As I write this, I’m in Hanoi with the ASEAN New Zealand Business Mission—two days of meaningful dialogue with Vietnamese leaders, including Vice Minister of Trade and Industry HE Nguyen Sinh Nhat Tan, on AI collaboration and digital transformation.


Ambassador Caroline Beresford’s leadership has been instrumental in creating space for frank exchange. Tomorrow we head to Ho Chi Minh City to continue this work. Building international partnerships rooted in trust and shared values matters—it’s how we amplify impact beyond borders.


As October closes, one thing is crystal clear: we must continue to champion human values, genuine relationships, and meaningful problem solving.


Reach out and let us know what technology skill you’re eager to master next.

Ngā mihi nui


Mahsa McCauley
Founder and Chair
She Sharp Charitable Trust

Last Event Update

Code Secure. Lead the Future: Women in Cybersecurity Workshop

✨Our October workshop at Xero’s Auckland office was a pivotal occasion in addressing the security and development skills gap for women in technology. In partnership with Xero and Secure Code Warrior, participants undertook genuine code vulnerabilities with remarkable determination and skill.

The session featured lightning talks from preeminent women, including Fatemah Beydoun of Secure Code Warrior, Suzy Clarke and Mehika Manocha of Xero, alongside myself. Each of us imparted practical career wisdom and encouraged the adoption of a growth-oriented, flexible mindset.

Sixty-two dedicated participants contributed to an event marked by camaraderie and high achievement. Their success has reinforced our belief in the promise of secure coding in New Zealand. We are delighted to announce plans for this event to become an annual tradition.

Ending the Year with Purpose and Resolve


As the end of the year approaches, She Sharp renews its commitment to fostering a future-ready and inclusive technology workforce. This November, we are broadening the conversation with two flagship events—one in Auckland, the other in Dunedin—each focused on empowerment, innovation, and exploring the changing landscape of work in the age of AI.


Seats for both events are limited, so do secure yours promptly. Let us end the year by building and empowering our community together.
