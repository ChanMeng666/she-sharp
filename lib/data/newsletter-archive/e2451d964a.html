She Sharp is partnering once again with academyEX for International Women’s Day 2026, creating a dedicated space for our community to connect, reflect, and take meaningful action toward gender equality. This year’s theme, #GiveToGain, serves as a powerful reminder that progress grows through community. We believe that when we collectively share our time, knowledge, support, and encouragement, we help open doors for women and girls—and ultimately, everyone benefits.


The heart of this evening will be focused on honest conversations and shared experiences through an interactive panel and Q&A session. You will have the opportunity to be inspired by powerhouse leaders who are currently transforming their respective industries. By hearing their personal stories of resilience and leadership, you’ll gain practical insights and fresh perspectives on how we can collectively forge a more equitable future.


Beyond the stage, this event is designed for deep connection and meaningful networking in a welcoming environment. It is a chance to be in a room filled with ambition and leadership, where you can meet purpose-driven professionals and show up for one another. Whether you are looking to build your professional circle or find inspiration for your own journey, this is a space where women can truly belong, build, and become.


Join us at academyEX in Auckland on 6 March 2026, registration starts at 5:00 PM and event kicks-off at 5:30 PM until 7:00 PM, to celebrate our collective impact and take small but meaningful steps together. We can't wait to see you there for an evening of connection and growth.

Agenda Highlights





Meet the powerhouse lineup joining us to explore how we can collectively #GiveToGain:


Ana Ivanovic-Tongue


Chief Delivery Officer, academyEX


Ana blends 25 years of experience across arts, culture, and EdTech to help adults build confidence in the age of disruption. A member of New Zealand’s AI Forum Executive Council, she is a leading voice on making innovation in learning practical, scalable, and human.

Danubi (Dani) Paim


Founder, Property Besties & Financial Wellness Strategist


After buying her first property at just 19, Dani founded Property Besties—New Zealand’s most collaborative property investor community. She is on a mission to help 1,000 women build financial independence and clarity without sacrificing what matters most.

Camille Elemia


International Journalist | Author | Digital Product Manager at NZME


An award-winning journalist with 15 years of experience in global newsrooms (including The New York Times and Al Jazeera), Camille now combines storytelling with technology. She is currently exploring emerging technologies and their social impact to shape the future of journalism.


Wyndi Tagi


Co-Founder, WE Mana & The Table


Wyndi is a business strategist and community builder dedicated to helping purpose-driven founders lead with mana. Through The Table, she creates global spaces where women can belong, build, and become while growing sustainable businesses.


Annette Rangi


GM - Digital Transformation, Heb Construction


A champion of diversity and inclusive leadership, Annette specializes in Agile Leadership and digital delivery. She has coached teams of over 150 across industries like energy, dairy, and health, always driving for continuous improvement and strategic excellence.
