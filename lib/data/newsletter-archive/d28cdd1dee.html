Industry Gossip 🗣️

Check out what’s been going on in the tech world lately…


👀 Anthropic CEO Goes Full "Techno-Optimist"
Anthropic's CEO, Dario Amodei, recently shared a massive 15,000-word essay championing the potential of AI to address global issues—from breakthroughs in biology to poverty reduction and better governance. Is this the dawn of a new, more optimistic outlook on AI? Read more on TechCrunch to dive into Dario’s vision.


📰 AI to Rescue the Ageing Workforce?
A recent New York Times article explores how an ageing U.S. workforce is driving AI adoption as a means to boost productivity amidst growing labour shortages. Despite only 5% of organisations reportedly using AI (a number many find too low), the article highlights AI’s potential to support economic stability—though questions remain on job displacement and worker retraining.


💻 Claude Takes Control
Anthropic’s AI, Claude, has launched a bold new feature, "Computer Use," allowing it to control a PC just like a human—typing, clicking, and even filling out forms. This experimental tool is still in beta and has built-in safety measures, but it raises fascinating questions: Could this mean a future where humans might barely need to interact with traditional computer interfaces?
