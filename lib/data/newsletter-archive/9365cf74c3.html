Introducing our amazing speakers!

NICOLE YUE LIN (SPARK NZ)
Nicole is a technologist and entrepreneur passionate about technology for social impact and diversity & inclusion. Currently a Data Scientist at Spark, she previously worked for Microsoft & PwC. Aside from She#, she's also involved with AI Forum, Philanthropy NZ & a regular mentor for innovation programmes & hackathons.

SHRUTI SHEREKAR (IAG)
Shruti is currently leading Salesforce at IAG. She has worked across High Tech, Telco and Insurance Industries focusing on improving customer experience and automation across products and IT tools. She took part in Stanford Summer School 2019 working on a project tackling the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

AMRIT KAUR (DELOITTE)
A Mechatronic Engineer graduate from AUT, Amrit now works at Deloitte as a Consultant in the Technology, Strategy and Transformation team. Amrit is passionate about diversity in STEM in all its forms and this passion is what fuelled her to start the AUT STEM Women club at AUT.

ZAINAB MANASAWALA (F&P)
Zainab has a diverse upbringing in over 6 countries, including the USA, New Zealand, and Singapore. She's currently a product development engineer at Fisher & Paykel, after graduating with first class honour as a Mechatronic Engineer from AUT. She is passionate about empowering women in STEM. Zainab is also an ambassador for Engineering NZ.
