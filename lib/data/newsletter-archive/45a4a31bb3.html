We’re closing in on the final stretch of 2024 and it has been such a great 10th year for us. From hackathons to AI events, we’re full on at She Sharp — so full on in fact, that we’re sending this newsletter a bit later than originally planned. Our biggest apologies!


August was an action-packed month for SheSharp! We kicked off with the fantastic "AI for the Environment Hackathon" 🌍🤖, hosted with AcademyEX, where brilliant minds came together to solve real-world environmental challenges using AI. Our talented winners will now present their innovative ideas at the 2024 Aotearoa AI Summit! 🏆✨

Exciting insights from Fonterra's AI journey! 🤖🤝

Last week’s event with Fonterra marked the second year of collaboration and was an incredible success. With over 150 tickets sold, attendees learned how AI is saving Fonterra 150 years of manual labor. Special thanks went to the Fonterra team guest speakers for sharing inspiring talks. The event was not only focused on technology but also on building connections and sharing experiences.
