#IamRemarkable Event

#IamRemarkable is a Google initiative empowering women and other underrepresented groups to celebrate their achievements in the workplace and beyond. In this session, you will develop the confidence to promote yourself in the workplace and in personal life, thereby breaking modesty norms and glass ceilings.

This program aims to:
1. Improve the motivation and self-promotion skills of women and underrepresented groups
2. Change social perceptions and refresh the conversation around self-promotion

CIO Awards

We’re excited to announce that She Sharp was selected as a finalist in the CIO Awards Community Tech Champions category for 2021! The CIO awards recognise the individuals and teams who have shown leadership, innovation and foresight in their contributions to ICT and business. The Community Tech Champions Award honours and showcases outstanding initiatives promoting digital inclusion with the goal of educating, exposing and engaging New Zealanders previously underrepresented in the ICT sector. Here at She Sharp, we’re so grateful to be selected as a finalist in the community category, and for the acknowledgment of our work in bridging the gender gap in the ICT sector. We’re looking forward to finding out who wins on the 23rd of November at the Awards Event. Congratulations to all the finalists of this year’s CIO Awards!

Inspiring Woman of the Month

Nicole Yue Lin

She Sharp Ambassador

For me, tech hasn't been a straightforward journey, it has been a series of zig-zags, ups and downs, diagonals, like a jungle gym. When I was younger, I loved computers. I've been obsessed with computers since I was 6. As I got older, I experienced comments like, “you don't belong”, which made me feel like I didn’t fit into the sector, like, you're a girl - what are you doing here. Those words do hurt when you're young, when you're still trying to figure out what you want to do, these words can really affect you. What's been important for me throughout this entire journey is my commitment to giving back to our communities helping other young women and girls who were in my shoes, who have doubted themselves, who had no mentors, who had no overview of what they could become, because I felt that was a lonely experience for me. I felt the pain and the alienation of not being included, and being told that I didn’t belong and I don't want anyone else to ever feel that way. That is my mission, which is why I'm just really honored to be part of She Sharp, being part of this awesome team of amazing women who are pushing the boundaries and challenging the status quo, bridging the gender gap. A lot of incredible people have come through and made this platform possible. I'm really proud that we are all pushing forward in the best way that we can to allow more women to be visible, to be heard, to be seen, to be included, to be able to move through the ranks, to be able to disrupt the status quo, so that we can create a more equitable inclusive and diverse future for all of us.

A message from MYOB: Shop local to support SMEs this lockdown

As New Zealand continues to come to terms with the latest COVID-19 outbreak, recent research by MYOB reveals why it’s so important that Kiwis continue to shop local and support our SMEs where we can. In MYOB’s COVID-19 SME Snapshot, 19% of SMEs revealed a short term lockdown would put their business under extreme pressure, while more than a quarter (26%) said it would put their business under quite a lot of pressure. In terms of the consequences SMEs feared would apply to their business as a result of locking down, more than two-in-five expected they’d experience a significant loss of productivity, 38% would need to seek financial assistance to cover expenses and overheads, and 25% would review their staffing levels. 15% would have to assess the ongoing viability of their business. In light of the projected and past impact of lockdowns on local business owners, MYOB is calling on #SheSharp members to continue to shop local and do what you can to support homegrown enterprises.
