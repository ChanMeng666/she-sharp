Our events in September…

Tech Women New Zealand: From Burnout to Balance

Our event hosted my AWS, perfectly timed for Mental Health Awareness Week, featured inspiring talks by Brigette McGregor-MacDonald and Maya Lee. They shared their experiences and tips on avoiding workplace burnout from perspectives of the mind and body.

 

Attendees had the chance to dive into strategies for maintaining mental and physical well-being. Key takeaways included positive task reframing, recognising and processing emotions separately, fuelling your body right, and embracing movement and light.

 

It was an engaging chat with something valuable for everyone to take away as we all work towards balance. 😊🚀
