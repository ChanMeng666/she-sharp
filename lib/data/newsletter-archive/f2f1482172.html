May Recap 📝

A word from Mahsa…

🌟 Kia ora  <<First Name>> 🌟

May has been a month of bold conversations, new perspectives, and continued global impact. As I near the end of my Fulbright journey in the United States, I’ve been reflecting on the power of community and storytelling to ignite change—especially in spaces where representation and innovation intersect.

At the IEEE Women in Engineering Leadership Conference in San Jose, I joined powerful conversations on how AI is transforming both engineering and leadership. We explored resilience, empathy, and inclusive innovation—reminding us that real impact comes from building solutions with people, not just for them.

In Charlotte, I spoke at a Women in CyberSecurity (WiCyS) event—an energizing evening of honest questions, bold ideas, and shared purpose. From imposter syndrome to ethical AI, one theme stood out: you don’t need to be an expert to lead—just curious and courageous enough to begin.

And finally, She Sharp joined forces with MYOB to host “Tech That Matches Your Vibe” in Auckland—a heartfelt night of storytelling and self-discovery. Our incredible speakers reminded us that our so-called “weaknesses” are often hidden strengths, and that every tech journey is uniquely valid. We were reminded that diversity isn’t just a value—it’s a superpower for high-performing teams.

Across all these moments, one theme emerged again and again: authentic connection fuels innovation. Whether you’re mentoring a young technologist, shifting careers, or leading with empathy, your story has power. Let’s keep creating space for voices that matter, embracing growth, and redefining leadership together.

Ngā mihi nui


Mahsa McCauley
Founder and Chair
She Sharp Charitable Trust

Last Event Update:

She Sharp & MYOB

✨ Tech That Matches Your Vibe, hosted by She Sharp and MYOB on 22 May, was an evening of honest reflection and inspiring stories. MYOB speakers shared powerful insights—reminding us that what we often see as weaknesses may be strengths in disguise, that our natural talents are easy to overlook, and that naming imposter syndrome helps quiet its hold. They highlighted diversity as a strength, not a challenge, and encouraged embracing the non-linear paths that define so many tech careers. Their experiences left the audience feeling seen, empowered, and ready to own their unique journeys.

Industry Gossip 🗣️

Check out what’s been going on in the tech world lately…

  • 🌐 Google I/O 2025 Unveils the Future of AI Integration

    At Google I/O 2025, the company showcased its deepening commitment to artificial intelligence, unveiling over 100 AI-driven updates across its ecosystem. Key announcements included the introduction of “AI Mode” in Search, offering users a conversational, chatbot-like experience, and the launch of Gemini 2.5 Pro, an advanced AI model designed for complex reasoning tasks. Additionally, Google presented Project Astra, aiming to develop a universal AI assistant, and introduced Veo 3, an AI-powered video generation tool capable of creating realistic visuals and audio from text prompts. The conference also highlighted advancements in wearable technology with Android XR smart glasses, emphasizing Google’s vision of integrating AI seamlessly into daily life. Source.


  • 💻 NVIDIA GTC 2025: Making AI Faster, Smarter, and More Powerful

    At the NVIDIA GTC 2025 conference, CEO Jensen Huang shared exciting news about the future of AI and computing. The company introduced a new kind of super-powerful chip called Blackwell Ultra, designed to help AI systems think and respond more quickly. They also previewed a huge project called Rubin Ultra—expected in 2027—which will connect hundreds of these chips to power massive AI data centers. Other announcements included DGX Spark, a small but powerful AI computer for workspaces, and a new tool called Newton that helps robots learn and move more realistically. NVIDIA also revealed plans to build a new AI supercomputer in Taiwan, showing their commitment to making AI even more accessible around the world. Source.


  • 🔋 AI’s Growing Energy Footprint Raises Environmental Concerns

    A recent study highlights that artificial intelligence could soon account for up to half of data center electricity consumption if current trends persist. This surge in energy demand is attributed to the rapid expansion of AI applications across various sectors. The increased reliance on electricity, much of which is still generated from fossil fuels, poses significant environmental challenges, including higher greenhouse gas emissions. Experts emphasize the urgent need for sustainable solutions and energy-efficient AI technologies to mitigate these impacts. Source.
