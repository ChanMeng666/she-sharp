Update on She Sharp

This month we ran a fantastic event in partnership with ANZ. We highlighted the experiences of women new to the tech workplace, as well as how people navigate the workplace during COVID-19. Our wonderful team of panelists shared a diverse range of stories, with a range of takeaways.

We also attended the Tech22 expo at GridAKL. Over two days we showcased technology careers to over 700 students. Hopefully we’ve inspired some more students to consider tech!
