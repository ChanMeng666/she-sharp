Dunedin is more than beautiful landscapes and historic architecture, it’s a city of community, creativity and future-thinking. Surrounded by dramatic hills and a long picturesque harbour, it’s one of the best-preserved Victorian and Edwardian cities in the Southern Hemisphere. Here, we see that a strong local identity and commitment to inclusivity go hand in hand with innovation.


This November, SheSharp and HCLTech, in collaboration with Dunedin City Council, invite you to: AI Empowerment: Shaping an Inclusive Digital Future, an evening where Dunedin’s energy meets AI’s potential, where technology is shaped by people and communities, not the other way around.


Join us for an evening of learning, laughter, and connection, where Dunedin’s heart for community meets the future of AI.

Agenda Highlights

Keynote Speaker:

Rasha Y. Abu‑Safieh

Head of Digital Strategy and Partnering at the University of Otago.
A Time Magazine Next Generation Leader and Edmund Hillary Fellow, Rasha brings almost two decades of expertise in digital transformation, AI strategy, and social innovation. Based in Dunedin, she champions people-centred tech that empowers communities and builds sustainable, inclusive futures.

Panel Discussion:


A diverse group of voices exploring how AI, diversity and work evolve together:

Graeme Riley

CIO of Dunedin City Council

Over 25 years in tech leadership across aviation, IT and public service, Graeme is now focused on inclusive, ethical digital capability for the community.

Deb Sutton

Labour Market Advisor – Ministry of Social Development

Deb has more than 30 years in training, recruitment, business development; she builds win-win connections between jobseekers and industry.

Kerry Ryan

Principal Advisor for Secondary Transitions (Otago & Southland)

With almost four decades in education, Kerry is passionate about culturally responsive practice, inclusive teaching and how creatives and tech intersect.

Sophie Wiltshier

Callaghan Innovation, MTF, PGDip, BA

In innovation leadership with 14+ years of experience across private & public sectors; Sophie supports women in business, scale-ups, IP protection and Aotearoa’s innovation ecosystem.

Abitha Maniyan (Panel Facilitator)

Vice President, Government & Public Sector at HCLTech A/NZ

With a rich background in financial services, management consulting and public-sector transformation, Abitha brings servant-leadership and real commercial strategy to the table.
