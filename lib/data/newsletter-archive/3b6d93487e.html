August Recap 📝

A word from Mahsa…

🌟 Kia ora  <<First Name>> 🌟


August has been a month filled with energy, momentum, and community spirit.

At Revved 2025, I was reminded of the power of showing up with honesty, energy, and a vision bigger than ourselves. Being on stage alongside trailblazers who challenge our thinking and ignite bold ideas left me truly inspired. Revved is more than an event - it’s a movement, and I’m excited about the possibilities we can build together.

On a personal note, I am honoured to step into the role of Chair of the AI Forum NZ, following in the footsteps of the incredible Megan Tapsell. Megan’s leadership over the past six years has strengthened the Forum and the community in immeasurable ways. As I take on this role, I feel both grateful and motivated to continue building on her legacy and advancing the conversations and actions that matter.

This month also brought us the AI for Good Hackathon, delivered in collaboration with AUT and She Sharp. Over 80 participants, 14 mentors, 11 volunteers, and a group of inspiring high school students came together to explore how AI can drive impact aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals. The creativity, collaboration, and commitment to real-world change were truly remarkable. Congratulations again to our winners, and thank you to everyone who made the event a success.

What stands out most to me across all these experiences is the theme of community and momentum. When ambitious, curious, and passionate people come together, ideas spark, learning deepens, and impact grows. Let’s keep that momentum rolling into the months ahead - because building the future starts today.

Ngā mihi nui


Mahsa McCauley
Founder and Chair
She Sharp Charitable Trust

Last Event Update

AI for Good Hackathon

✨The AI for Good Hackathon, delivered in partnership with AUT and AI Forum NZ, was an inspiring two-day event filled with energy, creativity, and collaboration. Over 80 participants formed 11 teams, supported by 14 mentors, 11 volunteers, and a group of talented high school students, all working on innovative solutions aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals. From improving access to education to tackling climate action, the ideas developed in just 24 hours showcased the limitless potential of AI for positive change. Congratulations to our winners, Team Robin, with Team LinkClick named runner-up and ThreadLight highly commended. A huge thank you to everyone who made this event possible—relive the highlights in our photo gallery.

🇳🇿Survey Shows Rising AI Adoption in NZ

A new survey from Adobe reveals that more than half of New Zealand consumers (61%) are already increasing their use of AI tools—or want to—and 75% are excited specifically about “agentic AI” (AI systems that act autonomously on behalf of users). This marks a notable shift toward embracing more interactive, AI-powered services in Aotearoa. 🔗 Read more

📚 OpenAI Releases GPT-5

OpenAI has released GPT-5, its latest AI model now available through ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, and API access. The model offers improvements in reasoning, speed, and accuracy across tasks such as writing, coding, math, and visual understanding, and introduces a system that adapts its responses based on task complexity. 🔗 Read more
