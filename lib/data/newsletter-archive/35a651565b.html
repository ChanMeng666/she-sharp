Shaping the Future with AI - Using Artificial Intelligence to Shape Your World
The world is constantly evolving. Digital technologies are transforming exponentially from day to day and Artificial Intelligence has changed the way we interact with the world around us. We can find information through conversations with machines, instantly translate written and verbal language and even have our own personal digital assistant.
This SheSharp Bytes episode features interviews with Isuru Fernando (Google Cloud Corporate Leader), Emma Shen Huang (Google Marketing Platform: Programmatic Media Lead), Hannah Weir ( Google Search Ads 360 Lead) and Ellyn Ricketts (Google Customer Solutions Market Lead) and will hopefully give you a flavour of the event and some insights into development of Artificial Intelligence as well as a taste of some of the experiences from female Googlers and their journey into the STEM.
