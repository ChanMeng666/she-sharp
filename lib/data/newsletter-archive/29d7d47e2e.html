Building Momentum Together: Pathways into Tech

A word from Mahsa…

Kia ora  <<First Name>>

April has been a month of building momentum around what inclusive, future-ready work can look like in Aotearoa – especially for women and underrepresented communities in tech. A big part of that journey for She Sharp this year is our partnership with the Ministry of Social Development (MSD), working together to open real pathways into sustainable employment, not just inspiration from afar. Through this partnership, we’re co-creating spaces where women can build skills, confidence and networks that translate directly into opportunities – from first steps into tech, to leadership in AI-driven workplaces.


Alongside this, our events are designed to meet our community where they are.  And this month, we’re shifting the focus to you and your career with “Own Your Energy with Candice Murray – Career Mindset Workshop”, in partnership with Metlifecare. This Thursday, 16 April, from 5:00–7:30pm at Metlifecare,  we’ll be digging into practical tools to manage your energy, strengthen your mindset, and show up with calm, confidence and self-belief in the moments that matter.


If you care about where AI, work, and inclusion are heading – and want to be in a room where those futures are being shaped, not just discussed – I’d love for you to join us.


Hope to see you at our upcoming Thursday event.

Ngā mihi nui


Mahsa McCauley
Founder and Chair
She Sharp Charitable Trust

AI Empowerment: Shaping an Inclusive Digital Future

The HER WAKA – Navigating Pathways into Sustainable Employment session, held on Wednesday, 25 March 2026 in Auckland, formed part of the “AI & the Future of Work” series. Delivered by She Sharp in partnership with the Ministry of Social Development and academyEX, the programme brought together up to 25 participants in a structured, high-impact environment focused on strengthening pathways into employment. The session emphasised building job readiness, confidence, and practical understanding of the New Zealand labour market.

Participants gained a clearer understanding of the evolving job market, with a specific focus on Artificial Intelligence and its influence on employment opportunities in 2026. The session provided practical insights into employer expectations, recruitment trends, and the skills most relevant in the current landscape. Participants were supported to strengthen their CVs, refine interview approaches, and enhance their professional presence to better position themselves competitively.

The session fostered a supportive and inclusive environment where participants engaged in open conversations and shared experiences. A panel discussion featuring industry professionals, alongside contributions from a guest speaker representing RCSA, provided real-world perspectives on hiring expectations and workplace trends. This created a space where participants were able to build confidence, develop communication skills, and gain clarity on their employment pathways.

The programme included a combination of panel discussions, a short hands-on workshop exploring practical AI tools, and structured learning activities designed to translate insights into action. Participants also received guidance on employment rights and opportunities to engage directly with industry representatives. The session concluded with networking, enabling participants to build connections and leave better prepared, informed, and confident in navigating their next steps towards sustainable employment.

#IamRemarkable

The April 2026 session of Her Waka brought together up to 25 participants for an energising two-hour programme focused on one powerful idea: that every one of us has a remarkable story worth telling. Held on 7 April, this second cohort session continued She Sharp's monthly employment-readiness series running from March to June 2026, delivered in partnership with the Ministry of Social Development. With a theme of #IAmRemarkable, the evening blended inspiration, self-advocacy, and direct access to industry recruiters, all within a warm, supportive environment designed to build real confidence for the New Zealand job market.

The evening opened with a 55-minute workshop facilitated by She Sharp Founder Dr Mahsa McCauley (Mohaghegh), drawing on the #IAmRemarkable movement born at Google. Participants worked through practical exercises to recognise their strengths, articulate their impact, and grow comfortable sharing their story with confidence.

A 30-minute networking activity gave participants dedicated time with RCSA-affiliated recruiters from Potentia and Elevate Consulting, including Abe Naus, Anabella Bianchi, and Sri Nanduri. It was hands-on practice in a low-pressure environment, exactly the kind of experience that builds real-world confidence.

Running from March to June 2026, each monthly session combines inspirational speakers, employer panels, practical job-readiness skills, and employment rights guidance. The result is a cohort of women who leave better informed, better networked, and ready to take their next step.
