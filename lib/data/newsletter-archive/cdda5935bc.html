Just a quick follow-up on our Women in Cybersecurity Workshop in collaboration with Secure Code Warrior and Xero, happening on October 3, 2025 from 9am to 2pm at Xero Auckland.


We know it can be tough to fit a full day of events into your schedule, but if you're still considering it, we wanted to share a little more about what makes this day so valuable. The whole event is designed to give you a supportive space to learn and connect, whether you're just starting to explore cybersecurity or you're ready to level up your secure coding skills.


The agenda is packed with practical, hands-on activities. The Code Secure, Lead the Future Women in Cybersecurity Workshop is all about getting your hands dirty with real-world scenarios. You won't just be listening; you'll be actively solving problems in a fun, gamified environment. Plus, the lightning talks are a rare chance to hear directly from trailblazing women leaders like Fatemah Beydoun, Suzy Clark, Dr. Mahsa McCauley, and Mehika Manocha.


To make the most of the day, all you need to do is bring your laptop and come ready to engage. No need to study up or worry about being a "beginner"—the workshop has options for everyone, including pseudocode.


We also want you to feel completely at ease and prepared. That's why once you register, we'll give you access to the Secure Code Warrior platform in the next few days for some free training. This will help you get comfortable with the interface ahead of time. Plus, we'll send a short onboarding video to walk you through everything, so you'll feel fully prepared for the workshop on October 3rd.


Spaces are filling up fast, so we hope you secure your spot soon! It’ll definitely be a fun day of skill-building and empowerment and we’d really to have you join us.
