Grace Hopper Celebration

Grace Hopper Celebration (GHC) was founded by Dr. Anita Borg and Dr. Telle Whitney, who were tired of attending conferences with low female representation in the fields of Science and Technology. They were inspired by the legacy of Navy Rear Admiral Grace Murray Hopper and wanted to give women in Technology an opportunity to showcase their technical skills and meet other like-minded women. It is the worlds largest gathering of women technologists and takes place in the Unites States of America once a year!

She# is proud to be able to stream GHC 2019 for the first time in New Zealand. Our speaker will be Social Impact Abie Award winner Dr. Nimmi Ramanujam who will discuss about empowering women to improve their health through tech, education and engagement. She has developed a unique framework that empowers women to help women eliminate cervical cancer. 

IamRemarkable Workshop

IamRemarkable is a Google initiated workshop aiming to improve self-promotion and self-motivation for empowering women and other under-represented minorities. The workshop will highlight the importance of self-promotion in your career and provide you with the tools required to practice this skill. This workshop will be facilitated by our very own Dr. Mahsa Mohaghegh. Through this workshop you will be able to develop the confidence to promote yourself in the workplace and personal life, thus breaking modesty norms and glass ceilings.
