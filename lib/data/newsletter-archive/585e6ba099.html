MYOB Kickstart Your Career in Tech

28 April 2023

MYOB Auckland Office


We had an incredible turnout with 80 people joining us for the evening. Kickstarting our event was a panel of incredible MYOB professionals Yvonne Weidemannn, ICP-APO, Lucy Brychkova, Anshu Maharaj and Laura Rutherfurd who shared with us their valuable insights on the diverse career paths one could take in technology.


Then Anna Dyer told us all about the MYOB DevelopHer programme – 2023 applications are closed but that means you have a whole year to get your act together and apply for 2024 when applications open! https://careers.myob.com/grad


After the delicious networking break Anna took us through LinkedIn workshop where attendees learned some practical steps to rock their LinkedIn profiles. And everyone made loads of new connections!


Overall, it was a great opportunity for everyone to network and learn from each other. Technology careers can be very diverse, and there are many ways to get on the pathway. From designers to quality assurance, product management, and beyond, the technology industry requires an abundance of skills. It is constantly coming up with new roles to make existing processes more efficient.


This event highlighted some of the routes that can be taken to build a fulfilling career in tech based on individual aspirations and motivations. It's important to look beyond the traditional roles of CTOs, engineers, and coding and consider all the exciting possibilities in this dynamic industry.

Listen to She# Bytes Podcast!

This month's #SheSharpBytes episode delves into our latest She Sharp Event, Kickstart your Career in Tech hosted by MYOB

MYOB introduced their DevelopHer Scholarship Program - aimed at bridging the diversity gap by helping more women launch their careers in software development through a paid scholarship as a full-time employee with MYOB. On completion of the course, participants receive either a Graduate Certificate of Application Development or credits toward a Computer Science Degree from RMIT. They then continue their full time employment with MYOB transitioning into the The Future Makers Academy. Anna Dyer  MYOBs Emerging Talent Program Consultant, talks us through the programme details and how to get involved.

We had the pleasure of speaking with Michelle Wong an Associate Developer at MYOB & MYOB DevelopHER Scholarship program alumna. We learn about Michelle’s Origin Story along with how she pivoted from a career as a Primary School Teacher to an Associate Developer at MYOB via the MYOB DevelopHer Scholarship program.

Technology careers can be very diverse, and there are many ways to get on the pathway. The MYOB DevelopHer Scholarship program might just Your opportunity to launch your Career into Tech. So get comfy and take a listen!

2 Sponsorship Spots Left – Don’t miss out!


As we continue to strive for gender diversity in the technology industry, it's important to acknowledge the role that sponsorship can play in supporting women in this field. Sponsoring women in technology organizations is a crucial step in promoting gender diversity and inclusivity in the industry. By supporting these initiatives, we can create a more equitable and thriving tech community for all.


She# (pronounced “She Sharp”) was founded in June 2014, and is a non-profit networking group for high school girls, women in tertiary studies and industry professionals.


Our goals are to connect technology students and professionals to ICT companies by creating networking events where they can meet. Companies have the chance to promote positions, programmes, or internships to prospective future employees, and attendees have the opportunity to make valuable industry contacts.


A typical She# event is hosted by a tech-related company and consists of keynote speakers who share their passion for science and technology, what brought them into the field, and the experiences, challenges, and successes they have faced.

Followed by practical sessions are also run to demonstrate real-world situations, and teach members valuable career skills such as management, leadership, and communication. Wrapping up with networking and nibbles.


The benefits of sponsoring women in technology organizations are numerous. Nothing is more motivating to girls who are either studying computer science or considering it, starting or have experience in the industry, than to see other women in the field sharing their experiences, challenges, and successes. Sponsors also benefit from the exposure and networking opportunities that come with supporting these initiatives.
