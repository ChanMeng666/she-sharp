💜 She Sharp isn’t just a community for women in STEM.
We’re becoming an AI-driven non-profit.

Events. Mentorship. Networking. Still the heart.
What’s new: AI now helps run our website, pairing people, Slack ops, and how the team publishes content.

Here’s what that looks like in practice 👇

💬 1/ Website chatbot (live answers)
Visitors can ask about upcoming events, mentorship, the team, or how to join — right on shesharp.org.nz.
→ Replies come from live site data (not a frozen FAQ)
→ When we add a new event, the chatbot can talk about it automatically

🤝 2/ AI for matching people
Two tools with humans still in charge:
→ Mentor–mentee matching: AI scores fit on skills, goals, personality, industry, and logistics; admins review before a match goes live
→ Volunteer / ambassador screening: AI scores applications and posts a summary to Slack for the team to decide

⚙️ 3/ Slack apps in the AI workflow
We don’t copy-paste event updates by hand anymore.
→ Event Bot: write in plain English in Slack → AI drafts a structured website update for review
→ Alerts in Slack: AI screening results and “newsletter draft ready” notifications
→ Weekly funding digest: crawls NZ grant listings, AI scores mission-fit, posts hits to a channel

🧠 4/ Custom project agent skills
Reusable playbooks our team (and coding agents) run inside the She Sharp repo:
→ Sync a Slack event-planning channel → website event page (copy, images, speaker bios)
→ Monthly newsletter loop: AI writes a draft → human edits → schedule send

And a public highlight we’re proud of 🎙️

For the Aotearoa AI Hackathon Festival 2026 (co-hosted by She Sharp), we built a Voice AI Agent — talk to a glowing orb, see live captions, get event answers by voice or text.
