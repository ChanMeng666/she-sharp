# Running the deck on the day

**Print this page and keep it with the laptop.**

---

## Ten minutes before you start

1. Open the deck URL in Chrome, Edge or Safari:
   **`https://www.shesharp.org.nz/present/<event-slug>`**
2. Connect the projector and check the slide fills the screen.
3. Press **`F`** for fullscreen.
4. Watch for the **"Loading assets"** chip in the corner. **Wait for it to
   disappear.** Once it is gone, every image and every slide is in the browser —
   the venue wifi can drop out completely and nothing on screen will change.
5. Click through two or three slides and back, to confirm the clicker works.

**Then do not reload the page.** Reloading throws away everything that was
loaded and starts again — and it will start again on the venue's wifi, in front
of the room. If a slide looks odd, press `Home` and walk forward. Do not press
F5, and do not close the tab.

---

## The keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **→** / **↓** / **Page Down** | Next slide |
| **←** / **↑** / **Page Up** | Previous slide |
| **Space** | Next slide — **except on a break slide**, see below |
| **Home** | Jump to the first slide |
| **End** | Jump to the last slide |
| **F** | Fullscreen on and off |
| **O** | Overview — every slide as a grid |
| **B** | Blank the screen to black, and back again |
| **L** | Stop all movement, for a slow laptop — see below |
| **?** | Show the key list on screen |
| **Esc** | Leave the overview, or leave fullscreen |

A presentation clicker sends the arrow keys, so it works without any setup.

**`B` is the most underused key here.** When the room breaks into discussion, or
someone asks a long question, blank the screen. A slide nobody is looking at is
a slide competing for attention. Press `B` again to bring it back.

---

## Break slides

On a break slide, **Space starts the countdown, and Space again pauses it.** It
does not advance to the next slide.

- Start the clock as you announce the break, not before.
- To move on when the break ends, use the **arrow keys**.
- If you started it by accident, pause it and let it sit.

The countdown is the most useful thing the deck does all day. A room comes back
on time when it can see the time running out.

---

## When you are running late

Press **`O`** for the overview. Every slide appears as a small tile; click one
to jump straight to it.

**Slides with a dashed outline are optional** — they were marked as safe to skip
when the day is tight. Skip those first, in the middle of the deck. Never skip
the closing karakia, the thank-you, or the feedback code.

`Esc` leaves the overview without moving.

---

## If something goes wrong

**A slide shows a coloured gradient with words on it** — an image did not load.
Keep going. It is a designed fallback, it is meant to look intentional, and the
room will not notice.

**The screen is the wrong shape, or content is cut off at the edges** — add
`?zoom=0.9` to the end of the URL and press Enter:

```
https://www.shesharp.org.nz/present/<event-slug>?zoom=0.9
```

That shrinks everything slightly so it fits inside the screen's overscan. Try
`0.85` if it is still tight.

**You are recording, or streaming, and need a fixed 16:9 frame** — add
`?aspect=16:9`. Without it the deck fills whatever shape the screen is, which is
right in a room and wrong on a recording.

**The laptop is struggling and the slides feel jerky** — press **`L`**. Every
moving thing stops: the drifting photo wall, the slow zoom on backgrounds, the
transitions between slides. Nothing is lost by doing it — the deck is designed
to look finished when it is still, so a stopped slide is the slide as intended
rather than a half-finished version of it.

Press `L` again to bring the movement back, and **the deck remembers which you
chose on that computer.** If the laptop's own settings ask for reduced motion,
the deck starts stopped without being asked — and `L` will turn the movement
back on if you want it.

One honest limit: `L` stops **motion**, not **loading**. The photographs are the
same size either way. It will make a stuttering deck feel smooth, and it will
not make a slow deck open any faster — for that, use the PDF.

**The laptop will not talk to the projector at all** — use the PDF. You were
sent one with this page. Open it and present from there. It has every slide,
including your host notes, and needs nothing but a PDF reader.

**You need the PDF and do not have it** — open the deck URL with `?print=1` on
the end, then print to PDF from your browser.

---

## The three things that actually matter

1. **Open it ten minutes early and wait for the loading chip to go.**
2. **Do not reload.**
3. **Space is the countdown on break slides, not "next".**
